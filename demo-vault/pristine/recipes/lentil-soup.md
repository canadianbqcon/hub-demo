## Ingredients

- 1 cup red lentils
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp cumin
- 4 cups vegetable broth
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 2 tbsp olive oil

## Directions

1. Soften the onion and carrots in oil, 6 minutes. Add garlic and cumin for 1 minute.
2. Add the lentils and broth. Simmer 20 minutes.
3. Blend half the soup and stir back in. Finish with lemon juice.

## Notes

Good with torn bread and olive oil.
