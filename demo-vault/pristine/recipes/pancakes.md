## Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 2 cups buttermilk
- 2 eggs
- 3 tbsp melted butter

## Directions

1. Whisk the dry ingredients. Whisk the buttermilk, eggs and butter.
2. Combine gently; lumps are fine.
3. Cook on a medium griddle until bubbles set, flip and finish.

## Notes

Rest the batter 5 minutes.
