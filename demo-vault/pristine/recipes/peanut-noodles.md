## Ingredients

- 12 oz spaghetti
- 1/3 cup peanut butter
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp chili crisp
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 cucumber, julienned
- 2 scallions

## Directions

1. Cook and rinse the noodles.
2. Whisk the sauce with a little hot water until loose.
3. Toss with noodles, cucumber and scallions.

## Notes

Great for a packed lunch.
