## Ingredients

- 1 lb chicken breast, sliced
- 2 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 red onion, sliced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 8 small flour tortillas
- 2 limes

## Directions

1. Toss everything but the tortillas with oil and spices on a sheet pan.
2. Roast at 425F for 22 minutes, stirring once.
3. Warm tortillas. Serve with lime.

## Notes

Skirt steak works the same way.
