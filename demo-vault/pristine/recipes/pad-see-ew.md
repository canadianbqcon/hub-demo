## Ingredients

- 8 oz wide rice noodles
- 2 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2 cups Chinese broccoli, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 1/2 lb chicken thigh, sliced

## Directions

1. Soak noodles per the package. Mix the sauces and sugar.
2. Sear the chicken in a very hot wok, then the garlic.
3. Push aside, scramble the eggs, add the noodles, broccoli and sauce. Toss 2 minutes.

## Notes

A wok or cast iron gives the char.
