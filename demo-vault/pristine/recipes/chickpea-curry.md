## Ingredients

- 2 cans chickpeas
- 1 can (13 oz) coconut milk
- 1 onion
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp curry powder
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 3 cups spinach
- 1 tbsp oil

## Directions

1. Soften the onion in oil. Add garlic and curry powder.
2. Add tomatoes, chickpeas and coconut milk. Simmer 15 minutes.
3. Wilt in the spinach. Serve with rice.
