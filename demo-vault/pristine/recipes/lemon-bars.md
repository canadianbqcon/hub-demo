## Ingredients

- 1 cup butter
- 2 cups flour
- 1/2 cup powdered sugar
- 4 eggs
- 1.5 cups sugar
- 3/4 cup lemon juice
- 1/4 cup flour

## Directions

1. Press the crust (butter, 2 cups flour, powdered sugar) into a pan and bake at 350F for 20 minutes.
2. Whisk the filling and pour over the warm crust.
3. Bake 20 more minutes. Cool fully before dusting with sugar.
