## Ingredients

- 4 large yellow onions, sliced
- 3 tbsp butter
- 1/2 cup dry sherry
- 6 cups beef broth
- 4 sprigs thyme
- 4 slices baguette
- 1 cup grated gruyere

## Directions

1. Cook the onions in butter over medium-low for 50 minutes until deep brown.
2. Deglaze with sherry. Add broth and thyme and simmer 20 minutes.
3. Top bowls with bread and cheese and broil until bubbling.
