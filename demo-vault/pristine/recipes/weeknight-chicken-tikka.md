## Ingredients

- 1.5 lb chicken thighs, cubed
- 1 cup plain yogurt
- 2 tbsp garam masala
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 onion, diced

## Directions

1. Marinate the chicken in the yogurt, half the garam masala, ginger and garlic for at least 30 minutes.
2. Broil or sear the chicken until charred at the edges, about 8 minutes.
3. Melt the butter, soften the onion, add the rest of the spices, then the tomatoes. Simmer 15 minutes.
4. Stir in the cream and chicken and simmer 5 minutes. Serve over rice.

## Notes

Better the next day. Add a squeeze of lemon at the end.
