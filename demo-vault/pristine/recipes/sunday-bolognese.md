## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1/2 lb ground pork
- 1 onion, finely diced
- 2 carrots, finely diced
- 2 celery stalks, finely diced
- 3 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 cup whole milk
- 1 cup dry white wine
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 lb tagliatelle

## Directions

1. Brown the meats in batches and set aside.
2. Cook the diced vegetables in the same pot until soft, 10 minutes. Add tomato paste and cook 2 minutes.
3. Add the wine, then the milk, reducing each by half.
4. Return the meat, add the tomatoes and simmer on low for 2.5 hours, stirring now and then.
5. Toss with pasta and a ladle of pasta water.

## Notes

Freezes well in 2-cup portions.
