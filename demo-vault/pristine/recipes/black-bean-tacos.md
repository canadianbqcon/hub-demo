## Ingredients

- 2 cans black beans, drained
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 8 corn tortillas
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- 1 avocado
- 1/4 cup cilantro
- 1 lime

## Directions

1. Mash the beans with the spices, leaving some whole.
2. Fill tortillas with beans and cheese, fold, and pan-fry until crisp.
3. Top with avocado, cilantro and lime.
