## Ingredients

- 8 oz spaghetti
- 1 cup finely grated pecorino
- 2 tsp black pepper, coarsely cracked
- 1 tbsp butter

## Directions

1. Toast the pepper in a dry pan, add a ladle of pasta water.
2. Add the pasta and butter, then off the heat, the cheese, tossing hard until creamy.

## Notes

The pan must come off the heat or the cheese clumps.
