## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 red pepper, diced
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 can (28 oz) whole tomatoes
- 6 eggs
- 1/4 cup crumbled feta

## Directions

1. Soften the onion and pepper in oil. Add garlic and spices.
2. Add the tomatoes, crush them, and simmer 12 minutes.
3. Make six wells and crack in the eggs. Cover and cook 6 minutes. Top with feta.

## Notes

Serve with warm pita.
