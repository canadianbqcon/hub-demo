## Ingredients

- 3 ripe bananas
- 1/2 cup brown butter
- 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1.5 cups flour
- 1/2 tsp salt

## Directions

1. Brown the butter and cool slightly.
2. Mash bananas, mix in butter, sugar, egg and vanilla.
3. Fold in the dry ingredients. Bake at 350F for 55 minutes.

## Notes

Even better on day two.
