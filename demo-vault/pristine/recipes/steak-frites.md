## Ingredients

- 2 ribeye steaks
- 1 tbsp kosher salt
- 1 tbsp high-heat oil
- 3 tbsp butter
- 2 cloves garlic
- thyme
- parsley

## Directions

1. Salt the steaks and bring to room temperature.
2. Sear in a ripping hot skillet 3 minutes per side.
3. Add butter, garlic and thyme and baste 1 minute. Rest 8 minutes.
