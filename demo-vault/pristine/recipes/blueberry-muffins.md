## Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 1 cup milk
- 2 eggs
- 1.5 cups blueberries
- 2 tbsp coarse sugar

## Directions

1. Whisk the dry ingredients. Whisk the wet ingredients separately.
2. Fold together until just combined, then fold in the berries.
3. Fill a muffin tin to the top, sprinkle with coarse sugar. Bake at 425F for 5 minutes, then 350F for 17 minutes.

## Notes

The high start gives the domed tops.
