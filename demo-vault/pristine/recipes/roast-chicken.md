## Ingredients

- 1 whole chicken (4 lb)
- 2 tbsp butter, softened
- 1 tbsp kosher salt
- 1 lemon
- 1 head garlic
- fresh thyme

## Directions

1. Salt the chicken and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight if you can.
2. Rub with butter, stuff with lemon, garlic and thyme.
3. Roast at 425F for 70 minutes until the thigh reads 165F. Rest 15 minutes.

## Notes

Save the carcass for stock.
