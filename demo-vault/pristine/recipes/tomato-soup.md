## Ingredients

- 2 lb ripe tomatoes, halved
- 1 onion, quartered
- 4 cloves garlic
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1/4 cup cream
- fresh basil

## Directions

1. Roast tomatoes, onion and garlic with oil at 425F for 40 minutes.
2. Blend with the broth. Warm through with cream and basil.

## Notes

Grilled cheese is mandatory.
