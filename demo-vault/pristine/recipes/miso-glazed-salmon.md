## Ingredients

- 2 salmon fillets
- 2 tbsp white miso
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

## Directions

1. Whisk the miso, mirin, soy sauce and vinegar.
2. Brush over the salmon and rest 15 minutes.
3. Broil 8 to 10 minutes until the top caramelizes. Sprinkle with sesame.

## Notes

Watch it closely; the glaze burns fast.
