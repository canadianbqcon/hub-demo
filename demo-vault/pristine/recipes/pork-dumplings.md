## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground pork
- 2 cups finely chopped napa cabbage
- 2 scallions
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 40 dumpling wrappers

## Directions

1. Salt the cabbage, rest 10 minutes and squeeze dry.
2. Mix with the pork and seasonings until sticky.
3. Fill and pleat the wrappers.
4. Pan-fry until golden, add a splash of water, cover and steam 6 minutes.

## Notes

Freeze uncooked on a tray before bagging.
