## Ingredients

- 1.5 lb baby potatoes
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp flaky salt
- 2 tbsp chopped chives
- 1 lemon

## Directions

1. Boil the potatoes in salted water until a knife slides in, about 20 minutes. Drain and let steam dry.
2. Smash each with the bottom of a glass on an oiled sheet pan.
3. Drizzle with oil and salt. Roast at 450F for 25 minutes, flipping once.
4. Finish with chives and lemon zest.

## Notes

Dry potatoes are the whole trick.
