## Ingredients

- 1 lb shrimp, peeled
- 12 oz linguine
- 4 tbsp butter
- 5 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 1 lemon
- 2 tbsp chopped parsley

## Directions

1. Cook the pasta until al dente, reserving a cup of water.
2. Sear the shrimp 90 seconds per side and set aside.
3. Melt the butter, cook garlic and chili, add wine and reduce by half.
4. Toss in pasta, shrimp, lemon juice and a splash of pasta water.

## Notes

Do not overcook the shrimp.
