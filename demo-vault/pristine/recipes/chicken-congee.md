## Ingredients

- 1 cup jasmine rice
- 8 cups chicken broth
- 1 lb chicken thighs
- 2 inch ginger, sliced
- scallions
- white pepper
- sesame oil

## Directions

1. Simmer the rice, broth, chicken and ginger for 50 minutes, stirring now and then.
2. Shred the chicken back in. Top with scallions, pepper and sesame oil.

## Notes

Add a soft egg if you like.
