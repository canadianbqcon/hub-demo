## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1/4 cup yogurt
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- 1 tsp maple syrup
- berries

## Directions

1. Stir everything but the berries in a jar.
2. Refrigerate overnight and top with berries.
