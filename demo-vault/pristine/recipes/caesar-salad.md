## Ingredients

- 2 heads romaine
- 2 anchovy fillets
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 egg yolk
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan
- 2 cups croutons

## Directions

1. Mash the anchovies and garlic to a paste. Whisk in yolk and lemon.
2. Stream in the oil to emulsify.
3. Toss with romaine, parmesan and croutons.

## Notes

Black pepper, lots of it.
