# Shift swap board

A small board where a shift swap is offered, taken and logged.

## Interview

### Goal

Sam: swaps live in group chats and get lost.

### People

Sam: six coworkers, all at one venue.

## Files

- Create `work/swaps.py` (offer, take, log)
- Edit `work/api.py` (two routes)
- Test `tests/test_swaps.py`

## Check

1. A taken swap cannot be taken twice.
2. A swap in the past is refused.
3. Both people see the change.

## Explicitly not built

- Notifications: he checks the board himself.
