# Candidate Profile — Job Intake Criteria

Read this before processing any batch job intake. Hard-cut rules are binary, with no qualitative judgment. This is a sample profile for a fictional candidate in the public demo.

---

## Hard-cut rules

### Location
- Chicago, IL metro, or remote within the US.
- Hybrid is fine up to three office days a week.

### Salary
- Floor: $62,000 base.
- Posted ranges are taken at face value; a missing range is a flag, not a cut.

### Driver's license / vehicle
- No license required. Transit commute only.

### Education
- Bachelor's degree is held. Roles that demand a graduate degree are cut.

### Experience level
- Target 2 to 4 years required. More than 5 years required is cut.

### Scam signals — any one of these = cut
- Asks for payment, equipment purchase or banking details before an offer.
- Interview only over text or chat apps.
- Posting with no company name or a free email domain.

### Obvious cuts — clearly not qualified
- Roles requiring a professional license or certification not held.
- Senior or director-level roles requiring more than 5 years.
- Roles that are primarily outbound sales.

### Flag for review — not an auto-cut
- Salary missing from the posting.
- Fully on-site roles more than 45 minutes from home by transit.
- Contract roles with a conversion path.

## Intake report format

One line per listing: company, role, location, arrangement, salary band, verdict (keep, flag or cut), and the reason for any cut.
