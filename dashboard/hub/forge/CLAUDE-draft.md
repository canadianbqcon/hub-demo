# Forge Draft surface (`/forge/draft`)

rubricVersion: 3

Spec: `docs/specs/2026-10-29-league-draft-design.md` (vault). This file holds the Draft surface rules and the rules that compute each trait. Bump `rubricVersion` here and `RUBRIC_VERSION` in `bake_traits.py` together whenever a rubric below changes meaning; every judged value is then re-judged.

## Draft rules

- The live draft comes only from the Windows poller (`lcu_poller.py`) posting to the Hub over loopback. No Riot web API, no auto-pick, no lock-in, no pushing runes or items into the client.
- The poller runs as Task Scheduler task `forge-poller` (at logon, restart every minute on failure), registered by `register-poller-task.ps1`. Its row is in the vault's `data/inventory/demo-desktop/manual.json`. A page stuck on offline: `schtasks.exe /Query /TN forge-poller`, then rerun the script.
- coachless and lolalytics numbers sit side by side and are never merged into one number. Mino's table and coachless data are never republished outside the Hub.
- The rune card's keystone is the resolved archetype's, so it always matches the seat's chip. Most-played is the base only when no archetype resolved or neither source holds that keystone.
- Enemy-team rune rules (`RUNE_RULES`) only nudge a page people play: a rule places its rune only when this champion-role takes it in at least `RUNE_FLOOR` (5%) of games under the page's keystone, the higher of coachless and lolalytics (`played()`). A secondary-tree change pairs the rune with the most-played rune of that tree in another row, never by id; below the floor the rule is skipped and says the share. (2026-11-02: Bone Plating and Second Wind replaced jungle Viego's Inspiration secondary at 0.3% play.)
- A build slot takes the best lolalytics slot win rate among items over `MIN_GAMES` (1,000). When none clears it, the slot takes the most-bought legal item instead (ranked by games, never win rate, which is noise down there), marked `thin`; the page draws it grey with its game count in the pill (Sam, 2026-11-04; Shen jungle had slots 2 to 5 blank). Off the pool, the coachless column reads `forge-stats-roster.json`.
- Counter swaps come only from his own newest saved set for the champion and role (`own_set`, never an autobuilt set), with no lolalytics slot gate (his "only in the custom build", 2026-11-05). After the base build (Mino for Viego, else the stats path), `set_swaps` scores every item with `build_styles.item_score` against the draft and swaps a set item into slot 3 or later, or the boots, only for a heavily skewed draft: one of its counter tags must read `SKEW` (0.85) or more, and its score must beat the base item's by `OPP_COST` (1.5), the price of dropping an item that serves the champion's win condition (Viego: kills); the build stays `legal()`, at most `MAX_SWAPS` (2). Each `threats()` scale reads near 0 for an average draft. A burst set (no armor, MR or health item in its Root or Core blocks) also caps defensive items across the whole build, Mino's included: 0 in an average draft, 1 when a threat a defensive set item answers reads `SKEW`, 2 only when it is fully skewed; extra defense is replaced by the next damage item in set order (Sam, 2026-11-05: "i am not a tank"; vs MF, Yi, Vayne, Irelia, Thresh no Death's Dance, Randuin's or GA, since killing self-peel duelists first is the counter). Sam, 2026-11-05: "you dont buy randuins into 1 crit character and 50% ad"; Randuin's is for a crit Yi, a crit ADC and Tryndamere together. Each item carries `counter {score, why}`; a swap carries `replaced` and the page shows both scores. Tenacity and cleanse score 0 until `tenacityCC` / `suppressCC` are published; hardCC never stands in (knockups ignore tenacity).
- ARAM: Mayhem (queue 2400) reads ARAM traits and archetypes for the pick lists, but its locked view is `build_engine.mayhem`: no rune card (the mode has none), no counter swaps, the build and the top three augments per rarity off `data/forge-mayhem.json` (`bake_mayhem.py`, metasrc's Mayhem page, headless, a fresh browser context per load or Cloudflare 403s the second). A champion metasrc did not serve falls back to the most-played lolalytics ARAM trail (its two-item core when the archetype is sparse, 60 of 173 on 16.21), labelled ARAM. metasrc's slug is the ddragon id lowercased, a two-word name hyphenated (`dr-mundo`); Jarvan IV has no page under any slug tried and always falls back. Checked 2026-11-05: OP.GG's Mayhem build is its ARAM build copied (same items in the same order, no game counts) and its augment feed returns only tiers 3 to 5, so neither is used; u.gg's Mayhem tables render empty headless; mobalytics has no Mayhem page. metasrc gives tiers, not win rates, and the page says so (Sam, 2026-11-05: "mayhem no runes, and builds").
- Scores read in win-rate points. A team gap is a heuristic, so the page says "gap", never a number.
- Traits live in `data/forge-traits.json`, one row per champion per trait: `value`, `unit`, `source` (`computed` | `unknown`), `basis`, `patch`, plus `raw`, `ability`, `number` on rubric rows. `archetypes[mode][champion][role][archetypeId]` holds the same rows per archetype; `champions[champion]` is the most-played archetype of the role with the most games.
- A value Sam corrects carries `by: sam` and survives `strip_judged`.
- Browsers run headless only, for every bake.

CC split (published 2026-11-05 on 16.22): `hardCC` stays, and `airborneCC` (knockups, knockbacks, pulls), `suppressCC` and `tenacityCC` (hard CC tenacity shortens plus soft CC such as slows) split it. Jarvan IV reads 1.5 s airborne, Malzahar 2.5 s suppress. Per cast `hard` is the longest hard CC word, so a spell that stuns 1.5 s and knocks up 0.75 s reads 0.75 s of tenacityCC (known undercount).

## Rubric rules (rubricVersion 3)

Every trait comes from a rule in `bake_traits.py` (`RULES`, 25 traits) reading the game's own tags, tooltips and numbers, and, where those say nothing, the wiki's ability text (`forge-champions.json`, the `text` lines only, never `notes`; approved 2026-11-03). A wiki-sourced basis starts `wiki <slot>:` and stays `source: computed`. Nothing is judged. A rule that cannot read its source gives `unknown`, a proven absence is 0 or false. `forge/judged/` stays on disk as history; `--merge` exits 2. Each rubric row stores `raw`, `ability`, `mechanic` and `number`.

| Trait | Rule |
|---|---|
| engage | best reach times hard CC seconds: the CC spell's range, plus a move spell's range if one chains into it. A CC spell with no readable number is named in `partial` and the rest stand as a floor. A stun that needs a wall takes no chained move and says so |
| peel | hard CC seconds from spells within 600 units and ally shield and heal per 10 s, each over the roster's top, averaged |
| dive | total move range over 10 s, each move spell times the casts the 10 s fight made (its cooldown rules, charges and cuts), plus a step each for `Trait_Untargetable` and `Trait_CCImmune` |
| frontline | effective health against half physical, half magic, plus self shield and heal per 10 s |
| zoneControl | radius squared times duration over zone spells, terrain as its own step |
| terrain | the spell tag in `TAG_FLAGS` |
| dashes, unstoppable, untargetable, stealth, pets | `SAYS`: a slot carrying the trait's tag, or whose tooltip, then wiki text, has its wording, with lookalike sentences skipped (on an ally or a target, a soldier's or clone's dash, a death or transformation state). dashes counts Q/W/E/R; unstoppable reads words only, since `Trait_CCImmune` also sits on Sivir E and displacement-only immunity; stealth and pets also read the passive; pets are the wiki's own "See Pets" list and seeds that sprout, never traps |
| spellShield | `Trait_Parry` plus spell shield wording, else the wiki naming a spell shield (Nocturne W, Sivir E) |
| wallCrossing | a move spell (tagged, or a `SAYS` dash) whose tooltip or wiki text names crossing walls or terrain, or a spell that ignores terrain collision (Skarner E, Smolder E); no such wording is `unknown`; no move spell is false only when the kit never talks of dashing, leaping, blinking or flying, else `unknown` |
| aoeCCShare, autoReliance, attackSpeedScaling | from the fight timeline. attackSpeedScaling is true when a calc reads attack speed or basic attacks reach 50% of damage |
| scaling | from the core items' stats, in the order support, ap, crit, lethality, on-hit, bruiser, tank |
| earlyLate | late minus early win rate off lolalytics' game length data, on -2..2 by quintile |
| resource | Data Dragon `partype` |
| cleanse, globalPresence, vision, revive, execute, infiniteStacking | a tooltip keyword with the quoted line, then the wiki text (a range field of exactly "Global" is global), else `unknown`, never false |

The 0 to 5 scale is a percentile: one row per champion-role, its most-played archetype, positive values only, ties take the lower score, proven absence stays 0. `calibrate` stores the boundaries in the data with `rubricVersion`; ARAM rows score against the same calibration. They move only on a rebake.

Reading CC: a `<status>` word takes the duration in its own sentence, `for` not `over` (that is a heal's length); then the bin's value named for the word; then, when no hard word read, `ccduration`, `stunduration` or `grabduration`. Reading range: `mCastRangeGrowthMax`, `castRangeDisplayOverride`, a DataValues name that means the cast's own reach (`_RANGE_NAME`), `castRange`. The 25000 placeholder never counts.

Reading a spell's damage (`spell_lines`, `kit`):

- **Largest line per kind, per cast**, because tooltips list one hit several ways. `@A@ plus @B@` is one hit and sums; the low end of a range and a `(max` or `(up to` cap are read, so an unread one still marks the line unknown, and not added.
- **A percent** is a literal `%`, `mDisplayAsPercent`, or a ref followed by max, current or missing Health. Max Health reads on the target, current and missing when the hit lands, and each (kind, health word) keeps one line. A percent of anything else (Grit, AD, a hit) gives no amount. A line with a flat part and a percent part holds one flat `base`, the percent read on the reference target's health.
- **Heals and shields:** a percent is left out, a bare value counts only when its name says heal or shield.
- **A charge spell** waits on its recharge. `cooldownTime` is only the gap between charges.
- **A channel** locks autos and spells for its length (`_HOLDS` on the text, or the bin's `mChannelDuration` on a spell that reads damage), and its cooldown starts when the lock ends.
- **A held charge-up** (`mUseChargeChanneling`, damage grows with charge per `_CHARGED`) locks for its full bin channel and reads its full-charge line: the line it reads pays the time that line takes, so a quick release reads a little low. Charge that changes only range or a stun stays free, except Viego W, Zac E and K'Sante W, which take the same lock and undercount for it.
- **`_WIKI_TIMING` is the one hand table**, for timing no bin holds. Every row names its wiki source: `lock`, `cooldown` (added whole in place of the lock), `free` (no lock, a roll or a release), `onCast` (wiki cdstart is on-cast, so the cooldown does not wait for the lock), `refund` and `back` (a share or seconds a cast earns off its own cooldown by landing or finishing: Fiora Q, Gragas E, Pantheon Q, Tahm Kench W, Fiddlesticks W, Ezreal Q, Thresh Q).
- **Stacks from before the fight:** a `BuffCounter` part reads `STACKS` in `bake_traits.py` by the bin's buff hash: labeled low, typical, high estimates at minute 15 (Nasus, Thresh, Smolder, Cho'Gath from community numbers and spell math; Kindred and Aurelion Sol from game-rule bounds with typical the midpoint). The fight runs on `typical` and the traits it moves read source `estimate`. A hash not in `STACKS` reads `unknown`.
- **`unknown`, never a guess:** a part on a non-mana resource (Fury), a sibling spell the bin lacks, a spell with no tooltip (Aphelios Q, E). The row's `reason` names what is missing.
- **Item timing:** `slot: "R"` on an effect limits it to Ultimate casts. A slot stack needs no damage (Hexplate), a slot on-cast needs a landed spell (Hatefog). Other on-cast effects also need a landed spell, except `afterHard`.

Known coarse, so say it when reporting trust: cooldown cuts earned by attacks, kills, crits or other spells stay out (Wukong Q, Lucian E, Miss Fortune W, Sivir R, Tryndamere E, Olaf Q, Janna E), and Ezreal Q cuts only itself, not his other abilities. a spell with a cooldown fires once per cooldown, so an on-hit part of it does not count per attack (Varus W, Kog'Maw W). Costs are ignored, so Kog'Maw R fires on its 1.5 s cooldown. A passive with no readable cadence counts once. A repeated line lands whole at its cast, so burst overstates a burn or channel (Karthus E, Annie R, Lucian R), while `damage10s` caps it at the next cast. Two lines of one kind keep the larger, unless the text chains them with "then" or "followed by" (Nilah R, Xin Zhao W, Viktor R), which adds them.

Known gaps in the rubricVersion 3 trait rules (hand-check 2026-11-03): pulls are not hard CC (Diana R, Mordekaiser E, Nilah R), so engage misses them; form-swap kits read one form only (Nidalee cougar, Rek'Sai unburrowed); Elise's rappel and Nocturne R count no dash, so their wallCrossing is unknown; Lulu reads no pets; Tryndamere, Varus, Nasus, Katarina, Aphelios, Bard and Hwei stay unknown on the SAYS traits because their spells do not resolve. The 2026-11-03 rebake took unknown champion rows from 1,309 to 1,227 of 8,131.
